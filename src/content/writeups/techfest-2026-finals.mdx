## 1. Incident Scenario

**Challenge Name:** VoltPN  
**Author:** ud1n  
**Category:** Digital Forensics / Mobile Malware  
**Team:** `POROS` (2byte, FAZer0, Araahi)  
**Date:** 27 Juli 2026  

> *A software developer noticed rapid battery drain and abnormal overnight data spikes. Sensitive confidential files were encrypted. Analyze the Android logical dump, identify the malware, and recover the victim's data.*

---

## 2. Investigation & Malware Disassembly

1. Extracting the Android logical dump reveals a suspicious package under `/data/data/com.voltvpn.pro`.
2. Inside `/data/data/com.voltvpn.pro/files/`, an encrypted container `locked.enc` was found.
3. In `/data/media/0/Download/`, we found the installer APK. Using **Apktool**, we disassembled the APK into Smali bytecode.
4. Inspecting `smali_classes5/com/voltvpn/pro/service/NoteCryptor.smali` revealed static cryptographic material:
   - **Transformation:** `AES/CBC/PKCS5Padding`
   - **AES Key:** `V0ltVPN_K1C4uwWw`
   - **AES IV:** `1a2b3c4d5e6f7890`

---

## 3. Decryption & Artifact Carving

Decrypting `locked.enc` via AES-CBC produces a PNG image containing a Google Drive link and password clue:

```text
the zip pass is:
mothers name_fav football club_cat name
(all lowercase)
```

Carving deleted SMS, contacts, and note records under `/data/media/0/` gives:
- **Mother's name:** `viona`
- **Favorite football club:** `barcelona`
- **Cat name:** `goolkie`

Password: `viona_barcelona_goolkie`

Unzipping the archive reveals the final flag.
